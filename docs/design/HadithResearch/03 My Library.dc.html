My Library keeps sources you want to come back to across all your projects. Saving here never changes the corpus, and removing something later never deletes the source.

  1. 01Save from the corpus. Open any book, chapter, section, report, occurrence, chain, narrator or judgment and choose Save to My Library.

  3. 03Organise later. Collections, tags, favourites and private notes. One item can sit in many collections.
