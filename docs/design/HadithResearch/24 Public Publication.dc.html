Kufan chains of the wuḍūʾ reports: where the “three times” wording diverges

Shilan Rashid · Aras Kamal

Abstract

Of 14 occurrences of the “three times each” report in the six Sunan collections, 11 preserve only the threefold wording. The 3 expanded forms all pass through Wakīʿ. We argue the expansion is a later development on that route, while noting the unresolved identity of his teacher Sufyān.

2. The wording of the Kufan reports

The shortest form is preserved by Abū Dāwūd through Musaddad.[1]

Al-Tirmidhī’s version adds the once and twice forms before the threefold one.[2] Paraphrase

⚖ Attributed to Ibn Ḥajar · Taqrīb no. 5065 [3]ثقة مكثر عابد، اختلط بأخرة

5. Limitations

The conclusion depends on an unresolved narrator identity and on one occurrence without recorded wording.
